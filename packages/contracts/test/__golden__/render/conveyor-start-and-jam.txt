Start C12 and handle a jam (conveyor-start-and-jam)
Tags: smoke, conveyors
Target: ${env.BASE_URL}, viewport 1920x1080, locale en-GB, time zone Europe/Paris
Policy: not strict, on break escalate, console errors ignored, screenshots to the LLM on-break, artifacts on-failure
Variables: conveyor = "C12"
Secrets: OPERATOR_PASSWORD

Steps
1. [s1] Use fragment login-operator
2. [s2] Open the conveyor overview
   Action: Click "Navigation entry that opens the conveyor overview"
   Expect: "Row of conveyor C12 in the conveyor table" exists
3. [s3] Start conveyor C12 (risk write, within 10s)
   Action: Click "Start button in the row of conveyor C12" (role button, text "Start", near "C12")
   Expect: Text of "Status cell in the row of conveyor C12" equals "Running"
   Expect: Colour of "Status indicator in the row of conveyor C12" is green
4. [s4] Trigger a jam on C12 through the simulator
   Action: Send POST ${env.SIM_URL}/conveyors/C12/faults with JSON {"type":"jam"}, expect status 202
5. [s5] Check the jam alarm appears and blinks (within 15s)
   Action: Check the expectations
   Expect: Jev confirms "The alarm list contains an active jam alarm for conveyor C12."
   Expect: "Alarm row for the jam on conveyor C12" blinks between 0.5 and 3 Hz
6. [s6] Acknowledge the alarm (within 5s)
   Action: Click "Acknowledge button of the jam alarm for conveyor C12"
   Expect: "Alarm row for the jam on conveyor C12" does not blink
7. [s7] Compare the overview with its baseline
   Action: Check the expectations
   Expect: Screen matches baseline "overview-c12-running" with a pixel difference ratio of at most 0.01, masking "Clock in the header", "Alarm list"
