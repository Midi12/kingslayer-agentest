Shift handover checklist (shift-handover)
Target: ${env.BASE_URL}, viewport 1920x1080, locale en-GB, time zone Europe/Paris
Policy: not strict, on break escalate, console errors ignored, screenshots to the LLM on-break, artifacts on-failure
Secrets: OPERATOR_PASSWORD

Steps
1. [s1] Log in as the incoming operator: Use fragment login-operator
2. [s2] Open the shift log
   Action: Go to /shift/log
   Expect: "Open entries section of the shift log" exists
   Expect: "Unsigned warning of the shift log" is absent
3. [s3] Sign the handover (risk write)
   Action: Click "Sign handover button of the shift log" (role button)
   Expect: "Handover signature line of the shift log" is visible

Handlers
- [h-relogin] When the probe auth_lost fires:
  1. [h-relogin-1] Use fragment login-operator
- [h-modal] When the probe blocking_modal fires:
  1. [h-modal-1] Dismiss the blocking dialog
     Action: Press Escape
